# Consequence Review Form

Blank template. Blank fields are unanswered, not agreement. Store separate copies
per reviewer and intent/pair; retain original responses before adjudication.

consequences from documented outcomes. Record unavailable context explicitly.

After independent ordering judgments, the coordinator may disclose v1's numerical
scenario floors and maximum-floor aggregation rule. Record whether evidence
supports their ratios AND aggregation, or only a qualitative ordering. Approval
of a flag is not approval of a weight. Do not supply a number merely to fill a box.
